## Lookup Status

Redwood identifies additional LOV-backed controls for legal reporting unit, catalog, transaction code, organization, category, item, trading partner, country, transport mode, and UOM. Those values already have Fusion/Data Loader-owned friendly-field mappings, or do not have a complete approved resolver contract. Do not derive a URL from a VBC service identifier, guess an identifier, or add another local resolver until its complete read-only REST contract is verified and explicitly approved.
